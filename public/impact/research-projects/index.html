  • DROPS: Dynamic, Real time, On-demand Personalisation for Scaling

  • FAIR-SPACE: Future AI and Robotics Hub for Space

  • ConTriVE: Control and Trust as Moderating Mechanisms in addressing Vulnerability for the Design of Business and Economic Models
