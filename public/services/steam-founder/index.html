A programme for your researchers

STEAM Founder

This is for you if…

…you are a researcher, technician or student with research or a technical idea to turn into a venture;

…or you lead research, a university or a consortium, and want your researchers to become founders.

CBIT’s flagship programme that turns research into ventures: an AI-first lean start-up journey for researchers, technicians and student founders in science, technology, engineering, the arts and maths (STEAM). Join as a founder, or bring CBIT in to run a cohort for your researchers.

Next step

Founders: apply to join the programme.

Email us about a cohort

If your email app does not open, write to cbit@ntu.ac.uk

What you get

  • An AI-first lean start-up journey, starting with technology readiness and research-to-impact
  • A value-proposition bootcamp
  • Business-model variants and growth hacking
  • Commercialisation readiness and data rooms
  • A pitch showcase, then a route into Venture Builder due diligence
  • STEAMfndr, the programme’s platform: matching with market opportunities is done by AI, and it matches research outcomes and competences with new product and service opportunities

Format

  • Cohorts of six phases of two sessions each, or a 5-day bootcamp
  • Cohorts run inside funded projects, or for the university or consortium that hosts them
  • Founders can join from an idea onwards

The AI edition

Turn research into an AI-native venture, with AI in every phase, from the first market scan to the data room.

Examples

  • PriVELT, the NTU continuation grant, EPSRC, 2022–2023. CBIT era
  • Train the Trainer, with CMU STeP, British Council (ISPF) and TSRI, 2025–2026. CBIT era
  • MOSMOP, British Council, 2023–2026. CBIT era
  • UKHAIC, the UK Healthy Ageing Innovation Consortium, BBSRC, 2024–2027. CBIT era
  • The Thai ELP, the STEAM Founders Experiential Learning Programme, 2024–2025. CBIT era

What AI adds

  • Delivered: for the UKHAIC cohort, AI is used to rapidly scan market opportunities, identify problem–solution fit, match partners and capabilities, and test and validate venture models; the programme included AI-supported data rooms.
  • Live: EASE’s Artist Venturing Labs run on CBIT’s AI-Empowered Venture Building method.
  • Owner-confirmed: in the STEAMfndr platform, matching with market opportunities is done by AI.

AI-native outcome: researchers who leave as founders able to run AI-assisted market research, test a value proposition with real customers and keep an investor-ready data room.
