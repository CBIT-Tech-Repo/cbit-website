About this website

Accessibility statement

A short draft for the CBIT Venture Builder website, written for these review pages.

About these pages

These pages are review mockups of the CBIT Venture Builder website. They are not yet published. This draft describes what the mockups do today.

The standard we are building to

The build brief for these pages sets the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA as the standard. The pages have not yet had an independent accessibility audit, and they have not yet been tested with screen readers.

What you can do on these pages

  • Skip straight to the main content: it is the first link on every page.
  • Use the menus, buttons and links with a keyboard. A visible outline shows where you are, and Escape closes an open menu.
  • Read every page and open the Menu with JavaScript turned off.
  • Pause the animation of the CBIT cycle wheel. It starts paused if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Use the pages on a phone without scrolling sideways.
  • Find a text alternative for every image that carries information.

What is not part of the website

The review toolbar at the bottom of each page is for reviewers only. It will not be on the published website.

Some links lead to other websites, such as NTU’s and social media. Their accessibility is outside this website’s control.

Tell us about a problem

If something on these pages does not work for you, email cbit@ntu.ac.uk and tell us the page and what went wrong.
