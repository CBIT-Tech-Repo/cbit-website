Every service, AI-empowered

Every CBIT service is AI-empowered, so the people and ventures we work with become AI-native.

  • STEAM Founder is an AI-first route from research to venture: in STEAMfndr, matching with market opportunities is done by AI.
  • In ImpactTracker, impact is tracked and analysed by AI agents.
  • TNDRX: AI profiles a company’s capabilities and matches them to live tender opportunities, so smaller firms can bid together.
